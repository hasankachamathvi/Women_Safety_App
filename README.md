Yuwathi is a mobile application developed to support and improve women’s safety in everyday situations. The main goal of the app is to help women feel more secure by providing quick access to safety features and useful information during unsafe or emergency situations.

The application allows users to easily report unsafe environments and view safety-related information about different locations. Users can quickly access important safety tools through a simple and user-friendly interface, making it easier to react during risky situations.

Yuwathi also encourages awareness by helping users stay informed about potentially unsafe conditions such as poor lighting, isolated areas or locations with previous incidents. By sharing this information, the app helps women make safer decisions about their surroundings.

Overall, Yuwathi aims to create a safer environment for women by combining technology, awareness and community support through a simple and accessible mobile platform
