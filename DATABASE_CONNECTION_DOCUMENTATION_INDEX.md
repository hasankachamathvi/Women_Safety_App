## 🚀 The Absolute Quickest Guide

**Just the essentials (30 seconds to read):**

1. **Open your app** → Log in
2. **Go to Home page** → See your name at top
3. **Long-click your name** (press and hold 1-2 sec)
4. **Tap "Run All Tests"** button
5. **Wait 5 seconds** for results
6. **See ✅ symbols?** → Database works!

That's it! 🎉
